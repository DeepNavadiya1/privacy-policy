QR Tools: Scan & Generate

Privacy Policy

Introduction

QR Tools: Scan & Generate ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, stores, and processes when you use its features, including QR code and barcode scanning, QR code generation, history management, exporting, sharing, and personalization settings.

Information Storage

The App does not require user registration or login. Your scan history, generated QR codes, barcode history, favorites, folders, folder names, settings, search preferences, and other user-created content are stored locally on your device using SQLite. This information remains on your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

Camera Permission

Camera permission is required only for scanning QR codes and barcodes. The App does not record videos or capture photos for storage. Camera data is processed only for scanning purposes and is never uploaded to our servers.

Photo Library Access

The App allows you to select images from your device gallery to scan QR codes or barcodes. Selected images are processed only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Local Data Storage

The App stores generated QR codes, scan history, favorites, folders, and other user-created content locally on your device. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Supported QR & Barcode Data

The App may store locally generated or scanned content including:

  • Website URLs
  • Plain Text
  • Wi-Fi Credentials
  • Contacts (vCard)
  • Email
  • SMS
  • Phone Numbers
  • Locations
  • Calendar Events
  • Barcodes
  • Other supported QR code formats

Advertising & Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Advertisement settings, including country-specific configurations and default ad behavior, may be managed using Firebase Remote Config.

The App also uses:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Remote Config
  • Google AdMob

These services may collect device information and diagnostic information according to Google's privacy policy.

Google Privacy Policy

Search Engines

The App can open scanned text using your selected search engine. By default Google Search is used, but you may choose another supported search engine or configure a custom search URL. Search requests are performed only when you choose or enable automatic search functionality.

Export & Sharing

The App allows you to export QR codes and related data as PNG, PDF, CSV and TXT files, and to share them using applications installed on your device. Exported files are created locally on your device.

Data Collection

We do not require user accounts and do not collect your QR code contents, scan history, generated codes, favorites, folders, exported files, or personal documents on our own servers. However, the App uses third-party services such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Remote Config, which may collect limited device, diagnostic, usage, or advertising-related information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
